## 📖 Overview
<a id="overview"></a>

**My Tribe** is a captivating management game developed by Grubby Games, designed for Windows users. In this immersive experience, players must help their shipwrecked tribe survive and flourish on a remote Pacific island. The **latest version** of My Tribe is packed with **full functionality** that allows players to explore, build, and develop their own thriving community. 

As you play, you'll engage in real-time gameplay that continues even when you're not logged in, ensuring that your tribe's journey is always ongoing. With a rich array of features and regular updates, My Tribe presents an unparalleled gaming experience.
